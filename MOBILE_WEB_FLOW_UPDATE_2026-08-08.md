# PawCruz Mobile Pet Owner Update

Updated mobile Pet Owner appointment and queue flow to match the web workflow more closely.

- Appointment is fixed to General Consultation.
- Pet Owner enters the reason for visit, then selects pet, veterinarian, date, and available 10-minute time slot.
- Veterinarian schedules are respected: Dr. Redmond Lopez (9:00 AM-5:00 PM) and Dr. Neil Norman A. Cruz (11:00 AM-7:00 PM).
- AI schedule recommendations are generated from available slots and can be tapped to select a recommended time.
- Booked appointments are Confirmed and can be viewed, rescheduled, or cancelled.
- My Queue uses the Pet Owner PawCruz header and keeps the web rule that queue numbers appear only after Staff check-in.
- Pet Owner menus now include Dashboard, Appointment, My Queue, My Pets, Messages, Medical Records, Notifications, Profile, and Quick Assist.
- Expo web export compile check passed after the update.
